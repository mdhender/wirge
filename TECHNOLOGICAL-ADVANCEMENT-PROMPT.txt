Source content the plan must cover (verify against the source — don't trust this summary):
- Nations start at TL 1 and advance through levels consecutively. A colony's TL caps the
  highest-TL unit its factories can build; a factory's own TL governs only output rate,
  not the TL of what it builds; once manufactured, a unit's TL is fixed.
- Three ways to raise a ship/colony's TL:
  1. **Research** — a build change order switches a factory group from manufacturing to
     research; it finishes its work in process first, then produces **1 research point per
     factory unit × TL per year**. Include the Research Point Requirements table (TL 2 =
     100,000, doubling each level up to TL 10 = 25,600,000).
  2. **Technology transfer** — an established ship/colony inherits its establisher's TL;
     multiple levels can transfer at once; a ship can only transfer levels up to its own TL.
  3. **Buying technology** at a market or trade station.
