Welcome to the documentation for **Epimethean Challenge**, a computer-moderated,
hidden-movement, play-by-email science fiction strategy game.

The goal of the player is the control of a star cluster consisting of 100 solar
systems. All of the systems have from one to ten planets each, but a maximum of
10 systems are inhabited; each of those systems having one inhabited world.

Each of the inhabited planets has a maximum of 25 nations; the players on a
planet rule the nations individually, and together constitute a "race."

Each game will have an entirely different set of locations for the 100 solar
systems, so that no two games will be entirely alike.
