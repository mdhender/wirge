Mass measures how much a unit or resource counts against the structural and
carrying capacity of a ship or colony. It is measured in **mass units** (`MU`).
One mass unit is set at 17,000 pounds — roughly the weight of 100 people. The
mass unit is the standard measure both of mass and of resource quantity; one
natural-resource unit is 1 `MU` of that resource.

## Storage

Certain units must be **assembled** to be operational and **dis-assembled** to be
transferred. A unit that is not operational is said to be **in storage**.

For which units must be assembled and the labor it takes, see
[Assembling]({{< relref "manufacturing.md#assembling" >}}) and
[Dis-assembling]({{< relref "manufacturing.md#dis-assembling" >}}); for the list
of units that behave this way, see the glossary entry for
[Operational unit]({{< relref "glossary.md" >}}).
