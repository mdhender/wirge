The current rules define a single victory condition: a **Nation Victory**. Later
rule books add two further conditions — **Race** and **Empire** victories —
documented below as future additions.

## Nation Victory

In order to win a Nation Victory, a player must control at least 100 planets, with no other player controlling more than 50% of that number, for four consecutive turns.

## Future rule additions

{{< callout type="warning" >}}
**Not in this version.** Race and Empire victories come from later rule books.
Empires are not yet defined in this version of the game.
{{< /callout >}}

### Race Victory

In order to win a Race Victory, a race must control at least 100 planets, with no other race controlling more than 50% of that number, for four consecutive turns.

### Empire Victory

In order to win an Empire Victory, an empire must control at least 100 planets, with no other empire controlling more than 50% of that number, for four consecutive turns.
