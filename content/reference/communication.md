Nations communicate through the **news service** carried by every home-planet
market and trade station, and through **diplomatic messages** sent with a
nation's turn.

## News Service

Every [home-planet market]({{< relref "trade.md#home-planet-markets" >}}) and
[trade station]({{< relref "trade.md#trade-stations" >}}) has a news service. It
prints:

- ship [departures and arrivals]({{< relref "exploration.md#ship-movement" >}}) at
  the planet;
- any [warfare]({{< relref "combat.md" >}}) at the market's or trade station's
  planet;
- a [trade report]({{< relref "trade.md" >}}); and
- any inserted messages.

An inserted message must pertain to the game and may not be of excessive length.
Messages are inserted with a news-release order (see
[Writing Orders]({{< relref "writing-orders.md" >}})).

The nations that may insert messages and receive the news are:

- home-planet [rulers]({{< relref "control-of-planets.md" >}});
- owners of [colonies]({{< relref "colonies-and-ships.md#comparison" >}}) on the
  trade station's planet; and
- ships visiting the home planet or the trade station's planet.

A visiting ship may use the news service only while it is visiting.

## Diplomatic Messages

A diplomatic message is sent in with a nation's turn and may be as long as
desired. It may be addressed to a nation's name or nation ID, a
[ship]({{< relref "colonies-and-ships.md#comparison" >}}) ID, or a
[colony]({{< relref "colonies-and-ships.md#comparison" >}}) ID, and it need not be
signed.

A diplomatic message may be sent only if the sender has a ship or colony at the
same planet where the recipient has a ship or colony. Once contact has been
established, the two nations may continue to exchange messages even when they are
no longer in contact.

{{< callout type="info" >}}
The rule book explains the lasting contact as an exchange of members of the
rulers' families.
{{< /callout >}}

Requesting [colonizing permission]({{< relref "control-of-planets.md#controlled-planets" >}})
from a planet's ruler is done by diplomatic message.

## Enforcement of Treaties and Agreements

Agreements reached through diplomacy are enforced by the nations themselves. The
game engine does not enforce treaties or agreements.
