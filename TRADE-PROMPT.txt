Source content the plan must cover (verify against the source — don't trust this summary):
- **Trade stations**: an orbiting colony the player establishes and controls, with trade as
  its only function; no factories but may have farm units; may be armed; can be conquered or
  lost to rebellion. Usable by any orbiting colony/ship at the same planet with the owner's
  permission (granted until retracted). All trade is in gold units; a station matches buy
  offers against sell prices plus a **1% commission paid by the seller** to the station.

  words "trade station"; minimum size 3,000 structural / 500 life support / 100 professional
  units, no maximum. Trade stations carry a news service.

  independent of player control, self-maintaining from commissions (and buying on their own
  behalf).
